Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

MarutiAI is a privately operated personal assistant. This policy explains how it handles data from Google accounts that are connected to it.

What it accesses

When an account owner connects their Google account, MarutiAI can access the services they approve on Google's consent screen. These may include Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Contacts.

How the data is used

  • Only to carry out tasks requested by the person the assistant works for, such as reading, organising, drafting or sending email, managing calendar events, and working with documents.
  • Not for advertising, and never sold.
  • Not shared with anyone else, except where the account owner asks the assistant to send or share something.

AI processing

To carry out a request, the relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider that powers the assistant. It is used only to produce the response to that request.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and working data are kept on the operator's private computer. Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the assistant's help to its owner.

Removing access

An account owner can remove MarutiAI's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Removing access stops all further use of the account.
